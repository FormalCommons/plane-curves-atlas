# Auxiliary material

Prerequisites that the atlas relies on but that stand on their own: affine
and projective varieties and their rational maps, following Chapters 1, 2, 4
and 6 of Fulton's *Algebraic Curves* [@Fulton2008]. Much of it is already in
Mathlib or Tau Ceti, and each page says what is. The milestones here count
like the atlas's own: the library must prove those that the libraries lack.

- [Affine algebraic sets and varieties](affine-varieties.md) (Chapters 1 and 2)
- [Projective varieties](projective-varieties.md) (Chapter 4)
- [Varieties, morphisms and rational maps](varieties-and-rational-maps.md) (Chapter 6)

These pages never depend on the atlas's pages under `atlas/`.
