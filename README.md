# Plane algebraic curves: an atlas

A map, in prose, of the classical theory of plane algebraic curves, following
William Fulton's *Algebraic Curves* (2008 edition). It states the milestones
that a Lean 4 library of the theory, built on Tau Ceti and Mathlib, is to
prove, gives their sources, records what matters for formalizing them, and
says what the two libraries already have.

Start at the front page, [atlas/README.md](atlas/README.md).

- `atlas/`: the theory of plane curves, one page per chapter of the book.
- `auxiliary/`: prerequisites that stand on their own.
- `atlas/references.bib`: the sources.

[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) holds the conventions for editing the atlas.
